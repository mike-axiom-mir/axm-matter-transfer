# Continuous-Adjacency Matter Transfer — Crosscheck

**Date checked:** 2026-09-02  
**Status:** speculative physics research / current AXM target framing  
**Claim ceiling:** no physical matter-transfer mechanism is established.

## 1. Current target

The September 2 framing is narrower than generic science-fiction teleportation.

The primary research target is **not**:

```text
scan source
-> destroy or discard source
-> transmit description
-> reconstruct a copy at destination
```

The target is:

```text
source matter remains matter
-> a continuous physical path exists
-> effective adjacency / path geometry changes
-> the same matter traverses that path
-> matter exits at the destination
```

`Effective adjacency` is AXM shorthand, not a standard physics term. In established gravitational language, the closest research neighborhood is spacetime geometry/topology and traversable-wormhole work.

The key question is therefore:

> Does physically admissible state-space contain a continuous path between macroscopically separated regions whose effective traversed separation can be made radically shorter while required invariants and causal constraints remain valid?

## 5. Physics crosscheck

### 5.1 Wormholes are a real mathematical research object

General-relativistic and modified-gravity literature contains many wormhole geometries. The classic Morris/Thorne/Yurtsever work explicitly treated wormholes as hypothetical spacetime structures and emphasized that their creation and maintenance touch deep unresolved questions in gravity and quantum field theory.

Source:
https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.61.1446

**AXM label:** EXTERNAL FACT — mathematical/theoretical research exists.  
**Not implied:** constructible macroscopic portal.

### 5.2 Traversability can exist in specific theoretical constructions

Gao, Jafferis, and Wall showed a specific holographic construction in which a coupling between two boundaries produces negative average null energy and renders an Einstein-Rosen bridge traversable. Their construction does not provide a terrestrial macroscopic engineering recipe and does not permit causality violation in the setup described.

Source:
https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.05687

**AXM label:** EXTERNAL FACT — a traversable theoretical construction exists under specific assumptions.  
**Not implied:** our universe provides the required controllable state or actuator.

### 5.3 Negative energy is constrained, not a free resource

A 2024 review by Eleni-Alexandra Kontou surveys quantum-energy-inequality restrictions on wormhole construction. Quantum fields can violate classical energy conditions, but quantum negative energy is constrained in magnitude/duration/averaging rather than being an unconstrained engineering resource.

Source:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.05963

**AXM label:** EXTERNAL FACT — serious physical restrictions exist.

### 5.4 The 2026 literature is still mixed and active

Recent Physical Review D work shows exactly why `PHYSICALLY UNRESOLVED` is the correct label.

**Construction/model side:**

- charged traversable solutions supported by bidirectional negative-energy null dust, with important asymptotic limitations:  
  https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/mtcj-13wm
- self-similar wormhole solutions supported by negative-energy null dust:  
  https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/kpgs-6gqq
- model-specific rotating wormhole solutions in Einstein-Maxwell-dilaton / phantom frameworks:  
  https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/mnkg-8ccb
- quantum-backreaction analysis of a special topological-wormhole geometry:  
  https://journals.aps.org/prd/accepted/10.1103/rqr3-6w4j

**Restriction / obstruction side:**

- no-go results for perturbative near-horizon wormhole construction within a class of effective-field-theory higher-derivative corrections:  
  https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/dsnv-gq47
- parameter exclusion regions for a quantized scalar field proposed to support a zero-tidal wormhole:  
  https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/h7d6-9yq4
- August 31, 2026 Einstein-Dirac study finding strong obstructions to obtaining a physically meaningful two-ended traversable solution in the studied system:  
  https://journals.aps.org/prd/accepted/10.1103/g89c-gqyf

These papers do not cancel one another. They study different matter content, geometries, boundary conditions, approximations, and gravity models.

**AXM conclusion:** the research landscape remains model-dependent. Mathematical solutions and serious physical obstructions coexist.

## 6. Quantum teleportation is a separate branch

The Bennett et al. quantum-teleportation protocol transfers an unknown quantum state using entanglement plus classical communication. It does not transport the source matter itself.

Primary source:
https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.70.1895

Modern IBM educational wording is explicit that matter is not teleported in quantum teleportation:
https://quantum.cloud.ibm.com/learning/en/courses/basics-of-quantum-information/entanglement-in-action/quantum-teleportation

**AXM rule:** MT-3 evidence cannot be promoted into evidence for MT-5/MT-6.

## 7. State/friction framing

The state-research work does not prove matter transfer. It supplies a useful question format.

For a proposed continuous-adjacency path:

```text
CURRENT PHYSICAL STATE
+ candidate geometry/topology/support state
+ legal transition constraints
-> candidate next physical state
```

Two questions must remain separate:

1. **Accessibility:** does nature admit the required physical state at all?
2. **Friction:** if admissible, what energy, matter, stability, precision, boundary, and control requirements separate us from it?

If accessibility is `0`, no amount of optimization solves it.

If accessibility is `1`, the engineering problem becomes a friction-reduction problem.

The present evidence does not determine that bit for macroscopic engineered continuous matter transfer.

## 9. Research gates for this repository

A candidate continuous-adjacency model cannot move upward merely because it is elegant.

Minimum gates:

1. variables, units, and geometry explicitly defined;
2. exact assumptions identified;
3. known-physics recovery domain shown;
4. stress-energy / support requirements exposed;
5. energy-condition and quantum-energy-inequality implications checked where applicable;
6. causal structure checked;
7. stability and traversability distinguished;
8. continuous path/worldline distinguished from reconstruction;
9. a quantitative prediction fixed before checking the result;
10. a falsifier defined;
11. alternative ordinary explanations retained;
12. independent literature comparison;
13. simulation success never relabeled physical success.

## 10. Safe near-term work

The current appropriate work is theoretical and computational:

- literature mapping;
- equation and assumption comparison;
- reproducing published toy geometries in simulation;
- dimensional/unit tests;
- causal/path analysis;
- source and claim registries;
- contradiction maps between construction and no-go papers;
- falsification design.

No biological, destructive, weapon, confinement, or uncontrolled physical-hardware experiment belongs in the current lane.

## 11. Current verdict

The dedicated repo is justified as a **speculative research lane**, not because matter transfer is known to work, but because the question is precise enough to separate:

- what mathematics permits under a model;
- what known physics constrains;
- what remains physically unresolved;
- what would count as evidence;
- what would falsify a candidate path.

The strongest present target statement is:

> Investigate whether physically admissible geometry/state configurations can create a continuous, causally valid, radically shortened path for ordinary matter between distant regions, and identify the assumptions or restrictions that make each candidate path valid, impossible, or unresolved.
