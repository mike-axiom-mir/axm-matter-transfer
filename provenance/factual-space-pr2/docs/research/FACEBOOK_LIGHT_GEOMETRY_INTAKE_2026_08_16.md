## Unit audit receipt

Using:

- `1 Mm = 1,000,000 m`
- `1 cs = 0.01 s`

therefore:

- `1 Mm/cs = 100,000,000 m/s`
- exact `c = 299,792,458 m/s = 2.99792458 Mm/cs`
- `π Mm/cs = 314,159,265.3589793 m/s`
- relative difference from `c` = approximately `4.7922511%`

Result: `c ≠ π` in the proposed units. More importantly, the numerical value of a dimensional constant changes when units change, so a selected numerical resemblance is not a unit-independent physical derivation.

## Claims retained as grounded anchors

- The exact SI value of the speed of light in vacuum is `299,792,458 m/s`.
- In geometric optics on curved spacetime, light rays are modeled by null geodesics under the applicable approximation.
- Maxwell’s equations support propagating electromagnetic waves and standing-wave solutions under appropriate boundaries.
- A field-wave drawing is a representation and must not automatically be read as the literal spatial trajectory of a photon.
- A physical proposal needs equations, variables, units, predictions, and possible falsification.

## Claims quarantined as speculative

- informational geometry as a fundamental transfer mechanism;
- harmonic reconciliation as a governing law;
- extra-dimensional standing waves as the established basis of wave-particle duality;
- light continually “resolving its position” as a defined physical mechanism;
- any path from these phrases directly to bulk matter transfer.

These ideas remain searchable hypothesis seeds. They gain no physics authority from being aesthetically coherent.

## Claims rejected

- `c = π` as demonstrated by the selected unit conversion;
- a photon literally experiencing all events at once;
- a sinusoidal or corkscrew illustration proving an extended photon path;
- a standing-wave metaphor proving an extra-dimensional structure;
- matter transfer inferred solely from light/geometry language.
