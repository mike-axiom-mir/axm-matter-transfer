# Matter-Transfer Physics Watch Anchors

**Checked:** 2026-09-02  
**Purpose:** preserve primary/near-primary sources that future research passes should revisit as the field changes.

These are research anchors, not endorsements of a physical matter-transfer mechanism.

## Foundational geometry / wormhole questions

### Morris, Thorne, Yurtsever — wormholes and energy conditions (1988)
https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.61.1446

Why watch: classic statement of the wormhole/causality/energy-condition problem.

### Gao, Jafferis, Wall — traversable wormholes via double-trace deformation (2016)
https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.05687

Why watch: explicit theoretical traversability construction using negative average null energy in a holographic setup, while retaining causal consistency in the described construction.

### Kontou — wormhole restrictions from quantum energy inequalities (2024)
https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.05963

Why watch: useful review of why quantum negative energy is restricted and cannot be treated as a free engineering resource.

## Current 2026 construction/model papers

### Dynamical formation of charged wormholes
https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/mtcj-13wm

Watch for: assumptions on negative-energy null dust, asymptotics, formation conditions, and whether later work reduces those assumptions.

### Dynamical formation of self-similar wormholes
https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/kpgs-6gqq

Watch for: regularity, boundary conditions, support matter, and stability.

### Possibility of the existence of wormholes in nature
https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/mnkg-8ccb

Watch for: model dependence, dilaton/phantom assumptions, observability claims, and independent follow-up.

### Quantum backreaction and stability of topological wormholes
https://journals.aps.org/prd/accepted/10.1103/rqr3-6w4j

Watch for: self-consistent semiclassical solutions beyond the linearized/backreaction treatment noted by the authors.

## Current 2026 restriction / no-go papers

### Wormholes as perturbations of near-horizon black-hole geometries: no-go theorems within EFTs
https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/dsnv-gq47

Watch for: scope of the effective-field-theory no-go result and what new ingredients evade it.

### Stress-energy tensor of quantized scalar fields in a zero-tidal wormhole
https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/h7d6-9yq4

Watch for: parameter exclusion regions, renormalized stress-energy conditions, and whether candidate support fields survive broader geometries.

### Obstructions to traversable wormholes in Einstein-Dirac theory
https://journals.aps.org/prd/accepted/10.1103/g89c-gqyf

Accepted 2026-08-31.

Watch for: follow-up work on the Einstein-Dirac(-Maxwell) claims, positive-frequency physical-state constraints, and whether any two-ended asymptotically flat solutions survive corrected treatments.

## Future-watch questions

A future research pass should not ask only, “Did someone build a wormhole?”

Check separately:

```text
new traversable solutions
new no-go theorems
energy-condition assumptions
quantum-energy-inequality bounds
semiclassical stability
modified-gravity dependence
asymptotic-flatness requirements
tidal-force constraints
causal structure
formation mechanism
observational signatures
state/worldline continuity
physical support matter
experimental evidence
```

If a new paper changes only one of these subproblems, record that specific change rather than promoting the whole matter-transfer hypothesis.
